# Proposal

Round 10: hired workers, wages, rates and upkeep.

## Why

Automation is how the estate scales. The money sinks keep the fortune honest: wages, the
seasonal parish rate and land tax, and upkeep.

## What Changes

- **Hire named townsfolk** as a field hand, dairymaid or stockman, miner, or carter. The
  carter hauls and sells in town.
- Each worker has a weekly wage and a skill that grows, and needs **housing on the estate**,
  such as a rebuilt cottage.
- **Assign workers** to areas and rows through the estate map.
- **Unpaid wages** lower their morale and her reputation, and eventually they quit.
- **Seasonal parish rate and land tax**, scaling with land owned and buildings restored.
- **Upkeep:** feed, lantern oil, tool repair and horse keep.
- **Unpaid bills** mean late fees and a reputation hit. She never loses the estate.

## Reuse starting points

- The estate map from round 1.
- The shops and money.
- Unreal AI navigation for workers.
- Coral Island and Stardew Valley helpers (conventions only).

## Smallest useful result and first playable demonstration

Hire a field hand, house him in a rebuilt cottage, assign him to water a row, and pay his first week's wage and the season's rates.
