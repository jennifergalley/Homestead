# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Confirmed outcome-level rules for this round. Expand them when the round is fully specified.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Workers are paid and housed
Hired workers SHALL require a weekly wage and estate housing. Unpaid wages SHALL lower morale and reputation and SHALL eventually cause the worker to quit.

#### Scenario: Wages unpaid
- **WHEN** she cannot pay a worker's weekly wage
- **THEN** the worker's morale and her reputation fall, and the worker quits if it continues

### Requirement: Bills never cost the estate
Seasonal rates, taxes and upkeep SHALL be billed on schedule. Unpaid bills SHALL incur late fees and reputation loss and SHALL NOT remove any land or building from her ownership.

#### Scenario: Unpaid land tax
- **WHEN** the season's land tax goes unpaid
- **THEN** a late fee and reputation loss apply, and she keeps all her land
