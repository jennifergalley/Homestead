# MetaHuman Store Clerk

## Why
Jenny's backlog (jenny-muu7idr3-mphjdv, 2026-10-04): "Instead of the placeholder heroine asset, the
General Store clerk should use a MetaHuman model with period-appropriate clothing, and it should be
a man in his 50s or so."

## What
- The general-store shopkeeper becomes a MetaHuman man of about 55, built in MetaHuman Creator from
  an Epic stock preset (Unreal Engine EULA), with greying hair and side whiskers.
- He wears original 1850s shopkeeper clothes made for his body in Blender: linen shirt with the
  sleeves rolled, wool waistcoat, long work apron, wool trousers and boots.
- He idles behind the counter and still turns toward the heroine when she comes near. The
  "(stand-in body)" label goes.
- Performance: one NPC, 2K textures, LODs synced like the heroine's.
