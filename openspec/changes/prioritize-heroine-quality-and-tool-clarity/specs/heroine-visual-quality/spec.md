# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Replace the disliked prototype heroine with a more appealing customizable character when a lawful compatible candidate passes real-game review, without making MetaHuman or a paid asset a prerequisite.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A better heroine is selected by a reversible game-camera comparison
The preferred route SHALL evaluate at least one zero-cost, legally distributable candidate against the current heroine in the actual woodland and an uncluttered close view. A candidate MUST NOT replace the selected build solely on an asset-store render, Blender still, metadata score or import success. The prior character and preview MUST remain available as rollback until a visibly improved playable result passes.

#### Scenario: Candidate looks better in normal play
- **WHEN** a candidate has verified distribution rights, valid import, compatible animation, required wardrobe/hair coverage and acceptable performance
- **THEN** matched in-game day/overcast/night and close three-quarter views can be compared with the selected heroine before promotion

#### Scenario: Candidate is legally or visually unsuitable
- **WHEN** the asset's license, access, body/garment fit, expected customization, rendering or actual game appearance is inadequate
- **THEN** it remains a rejected trial; no purchase, new account, silent asset substitution, or change to the selected preview occurs

### Requirement: Appearance choices remain real and complete
A replacement heroine SHALL retain the current supported adult body presets, hairstyle choices including straight bob and long waves, hair/skin/eye controls, owned tunic/apron/footwear presentation, and saved look/garment identity. Every visible choice MUST resolve a real compatible asset or material; an unsupported combination MUST NOT silently become a generic face, missing garment, or technical stand-in.

#### Scenario: Reopen a saved look
- **WHEN** a look and worn clothing are saved, the game is restarted, and that save is loaded
- **THEN** the same selected body, face treatment, hair/color and owned/dyed garments render without duplicated or missing clothing

#### Scenario: Change bodies and outfits
- **WHEN** the player cycles all currently supported body types and representative hair/garment combinations
- **THEN** the heroine remains fully clothed, fitted, shaded and animated with no exposed seams or old-mesh fallback represented as the new character

### Requirement: Non-MetaHuman quality has an honest acceptance boundary
The new heroine SHALL improve face/body proportions, skin and eye shading, hair silhouette/edges, garment fit and movement at ordinary third-person distance. Close views SHALL expose defects rather than hide them behind a backdrop or lighting trick. The project MUST NOT claim MetaHuman-grade photorealism or final character approval without Jenny's actual review.

#### Scenario: No external candidate meets the gate
- **WHEN** all available zero-cost distributable replacements fail compatibility or visual comparison
- **THEN** substantial re-authoring of the admitted CC0 base remains an allowed fallback, but mere lighting or recoloring without an observable face/silhouette improvement does not satisfy this change
