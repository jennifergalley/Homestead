# Proposal

Round 6: ranching and the livestock dealer.

## Why

Ranching is one of the three core loops: raising animals bought young, tending them gently,
and selling their milk, eggs and wool, with no slaughter.

## What Changes

- **Livestock dealer** in town, selling chicks, ducklings, calves, kids (goats), lambs and
  piglets, and buying animals back.
- **Player-built facilities:** fences and gates, pens, a coop, a barn and a pig sty, placed
  inside the estate.
- **Gentle care:**
  - Feeding with hay (cut in round 1) or grazing a fenced pasture in season.
  - Petting, brushing and letting the animals out.
  - Neglect lowers happiness and output, and animals never die.
  - Each animal has a name and a friendship level that improves its products.
- **Products:** milk from cows and goats, eggs from chickens and ducks, wool from sheep, and
  truffles found by pigs. They sell at the general store for now; artisan processing comes in
  round 9.
- **No slaughter.** Meat comes only from the town butcher.

## Reuse starting points

- The existing fence and structure placement.
- The catalogue and shops.
- Hay from round 1.
- Coral Island and Stardew Valley animal care (conventions only).
- Licensed free animal meshes and animations where the terms verify, otherwise Blender-authored.

## Smallest useful result and first playable demonstration

Buy two chicks, build a coop and fenced run, feed and pet them until they're grown, and collect and sell their eggs.
