# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Confirmed outcome-level rules for this round. Expand them when the round is fully specified.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Livestock care is gentle and never lethal
Livestock SHALL never die. Neglect SHALL only lower happiness and output. Livestock SHALL NOT be slaughtered and SHALL be sellable back to the livestock dealer.

#### Scenario: Missed feeding
- **WHEN** she forgets to feed her chickens for three days
- **THEN** they are unhappy and lay fewer eggs, and none die
