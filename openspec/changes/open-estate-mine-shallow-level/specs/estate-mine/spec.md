# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Confirmed outcome-level rules for this round. Expand them when the round is fully specified.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The mine is a fixed maze with gentle danger
The mine SHALL be a fixed, hand-authored tunnel network. Running out of lantern light SHALL leave near-darkness rather than harm, and exhaustion underground SHALL return her to the surface with some ore lost and no other harm.

#### Scenario: Lantern runs dry
- **WHEN** her lantern oil runs out on the shallow level
- **THEN** the tunnels become near-dark, and her chalk marks remain visible enough to follow out

### Requirement: Ore and gems follow the simplified ladder
Mine output SHALL follow the ore ladder stone, coal, copper, iron, silver, gold, and the gem ladder quartz, amethyst, topaz, emerald, ruby, diamond, with better finds deeper. Ores SHALL smelt into bars, and both SHALL sell.

#### Scenario: Shallow finds
- **WHEN** she mines veins on the shallow level
- **THEN** she finds only stone, coal and copper ore, plus occasional quartz or amethyst
