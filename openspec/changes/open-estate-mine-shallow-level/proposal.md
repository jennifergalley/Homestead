# Proposal

Round 7: reopen the estate mine's shallow level.

## Why

The derelict mine is the long-term fortune: the most lucrative and hardest loop. Round 7 clears
its collapsed entrance and opens the shallow level.

## What Changes

- **Restore the entrance.** Clear the collapsed adit and shore it with timber. It's a staged
  restoration with material costs.
- **Shallow level:**
  - A fixed, hand-authored tunnel network: a maze you learn, with chalk marks you place.
  - The **pickaxe** mines stone, coal and copper veins, and veins regrow over time.
  - Quartz and amethyst pockets appear at random within known veins.
- **Lantern and light:**
  - Lantern oil is consumed while underground.
  - When it runs out it's near-dark, and she follows her chalk marks out.
- **Gentle danger:**
  - Collapsing from exhaustion means waking at the surface minus some ore.
  - Rockfalls block tunnels until they're shored.
- **Furnace** on the estate smelts ore into bars. Ores and bars both sell.
- **Smelting house** buys ores and bars, and the **jeweller** buys gems.
- Steel tool upgrades at the blacksmith need smelted bars.

## Reuse starting points

- The existing pickaxe and rock clearing from round 1.
- Unreal lighting for the lantern.
- Stardew Valley and Coral Island mine loops (conventions only).
- Period Cornish mining references for the look of adits and shoring.

## Smallest useful result and first playable demonstration

Shore the entrance, go down with a lit lantern, chalk a route, mine copper and find an amethyst, climb out, smelt a bar, and sell ore and the gem in town.
