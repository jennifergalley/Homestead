# Proposal

Round 5: dismantle and freely rebuild the manor, decorate it, and bring home the dog.

## Why

The ruin marks where the family's manor stood. Jenny wants to take it apart and rebuild it
wall by wall to her own design, then decorate the interior with things she's crafted or bought.
The dog companion lives there.

## What Changes

- **Dismantle the ruin.** Replace the round-1 set with dismantlable Simulation pieces. Each
  one yields salvage: stone, slate, timber and ironwork.
- **Freeform rebuild** on the old footprint with the existing foundation and wall snapping:
  - New granite, brick and timber wall skins, stairs and upper floors.
  - Sash windows and doors, and slate roofing.
  - Pieces cost planks, stone, and bought brick, slate, glass, lime mortar and ironwork.
- **The standing room** becomes dismantlable and can be absorbed into the new house.
- **Interior décor:**
  - Free placement of furniture and décor, crafted at the workbench or bought from the
    cabinetmaker.
  - Rugs and wallpaper, and grid-free rotation.
- **Dog companion:**
  - A pit bull or Great Dane, with coats including blue-nose grey and merle, and a name.
  - Pet, feed and bond with the dog, and have it follow on outings or stay home.
  - It's never harmed or lost.

## Reuse starting points

- The existing snapped building and piece system.
- Round 1's ruin kit, which is modular by design.
- The workbench.
- The cabinetmaker shop.
- Coral Island, Dreamlight Valley and Stardew Valley décor placement (conventions only).
- Freely licensed dog animation sets, checked for licence.

## Smallest useful result and first playable demonstration

Dismantle one ruined wing for salvage, build a new room on its footprint, furnish it with a bought table and a crafted chair, and have the dog greet her at the door.
