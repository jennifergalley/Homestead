- [x] 5.4 Fix the head-and-shoulder wobble Jenny saw in play (2026-09-25: "the line that goes through her model is spinning / wobbling"). (Cause: the retargeter's Root Motion op wrote a root that snaked ±3 cm and yawed -5 to +16° every step; the game locks the root, so the whole body rocked and swayed. `gasp_locomotion.build_retargeter` now leaves that op off, and `straighten_root` moves the travel onto a straight, unrotated root. In play the walk's lateral head sway dropped from 35.6 to 8.1 cm peak to peak, and pelvis sway from 22.2 to 7.4, which matches the source clip. `InferMeshYaw` also averages both feet, so the MetaHuman faces her direction of travel; she had been 10° off because of toe-out. Walk and run clip speeds are now 209.9 and 524.8 cm/s.)
- [x] 5.5 Fix the footstep sound (Jenny, 2026-09-25: step rate doubled, too loud, doesn't sound like bare feet on soft dirt). (Cause: steps fired every 70 cm, which the legacy heroine's short stride matched. The MetaHuman's stride is 105 cm walking and about 160 cm sprinting, so the sound ran about 1.5x too fast walking and over 2x sprinting. The Kenney grass step is also a shod heel click: 0.7 ms attack and a peak above 0 dBFS. Now `gasp_locomotion.add_footstep_notifies` puts a `UHomesteadFootstepNotify` on every touchdown in the GASP clips. The notify only sounds while its clip dominates the blend, with an 0.18 s guard. PIE log: walk every 0.50 s and sprint every 0.32-0.39 s, alternating L/R. The samples are original synthesized bare-foot-on-soil steps from `Scripts/generate_bare_footsteps.py`: 6 heel-then-ball walk variants and 4 forefoot run variants, with 6-16 ms attacks and nothing above 3 kHz. They play about 10 dB quieter than before. Jenny to judge the sound by ear.)
