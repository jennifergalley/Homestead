# Design

Each meal gets an appended `SHomesteadIcon::EKind` after the seed kinds, so the existing `>= SeedRoots` glyph-first rule covers it and the photo icon is never loaded. Shared helpers (bowl, crock, plate, plank, steam) keep one ink weight; hot dishes show at most two steam wisps. Compotes sit in crocks, the hotpot is lidded, mackerel slices sit on a board, and grilled trout and perch keep their species markings under grill lines. Meshes and `HomesteadOriginalItemArt` entries stay for world servings.
