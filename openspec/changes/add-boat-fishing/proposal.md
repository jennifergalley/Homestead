# Proposal

Round 13: boat fishing for pilchard and crab.

## Why

Pilchard fishing was a mainstay of the period Cornish economy. A boat turns fishing into a
fourth lucrative loop.

## What Changes

- **Buy or build a boat**, moored at the estate cove or the harbour.
- **Row or sail** in a bounded area of coastal water.
- **Pilchard seine netting** in season, and **crab and lobster pots** set and checked daily.
- **Catches** sell to the fishmonger, or are preserved: salted pilchards and potted crab.

## Reuse starting points

- Round 1's ocean.
- Round 4's fishing.
- Water-plugin buoyancy, or a simple kinematic boat.

## Smallest useful result and first playable demonstration

Row out from the cove, set three crab pots, return the next day to haul them, and sell the catch.
