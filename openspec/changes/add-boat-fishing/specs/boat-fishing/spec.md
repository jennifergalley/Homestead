## Purpose

Confirmed outcome-level rules for this round. Expand them when the round is fully specified.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Boat fishing is a lucrative seasonal loop
A boat SHALL give access to pilchard netting in season and to crab and lobster pots checked daily, with catches that store without spoiling and sell to the fishmonger.

#### Scenario: Haul pots
- **WHEN** she returns to her crab pots the day after setting them
- **THEN** the pots hold catches that she can bring ashore and sell
