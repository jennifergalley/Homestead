## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Night stays night
After dusk the moonlit ground SHALL NOT brighten as the night goes on. Displayed moonlit ground SHALL
sit roughly 1.5 to 3.5 stops under an adapted daylight grey at 21:00, midnight and 03:00, and the light
SHALL change without visible pops.

#### Scenario: Evening into night
- **WHEN** the clock runs from 18:50 to midnight on a clear night
- **THEN** the scene only darkens or holds, and 21:00 is no brighter than 18:45
