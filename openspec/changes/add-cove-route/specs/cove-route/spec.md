# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: An on-foot route from the manor to the cove
The estate SHALL have a continuous route she can walk from outside the manor's front door on its south
front to the sand at the head of the cove, drawn on the estate map as a dashed footpath and worn into the
ground.

#### Scenario: Walking down to the beach
- **WHEN** she leaves the manor by the front door and follows the fingerpost
- **THEN** she reaches the sand at the head of the cove on foot, without sliding, jumping or leaving the path

### Requirement: Paths no steeper than 1 in 7
Every stretch of the route that isn't steps SHALL be a graded path no steeper than 1 in 7 over any 2 m.

#### Scenario: The meadow switchbacks
- **WHEN** she walks the path down the manor's south slope
- **THEN** it falls at 1 in 7 or gentler, through three switchbacks, with kerbs where the ground falls away

### Requirement: The steep descent is on railed steps
Anything steeper than a path SHALL be on granite steps within Props' kit (rise 15-17 cm, going 30-35 cm,
at most 12 risers to a flight, landings of at least 1.2 m, pitched within a degree of a 26, 28 or 30 degree
rail bay), each flight railed on one side for its whole length, with the ground cut at least 5 cm under
every tread and landing. The final descent to the beach SHALL never be an exposed dirt slope.

#### Scenario: The cliff steps
- **WHEN** she reaches the head of the valley side
- **THEN** a fingerpost points her down flights of granite steps with a rail beside her, turning on landings

### Requirement: Clear of the river, trees and forage
The route SHALL keep 6 m or more off the river's centreline, 2 m or more from the estate's felling trees,
and nothing she can pick or fell SHALL stand on its steps. Rails and kerbs SHALL stand outside the 1.4 m
clear width of every part of the route.

#### Scenario: The hairpin at the bench steps
- **WHEN** the path doubles back at the foot of a flight
- **THEN** the flight's rail stands on its other side and never across the path
