# Modern clothing batch

## Why

Jenny, 2026-10-09: "design me several more colored and various designs of different shirts, coats,
bottoms and shoes for her to wear? I'd like a variety of modern-looking clothes - never mind the time
period. I'd like to review each one before it goes in the game" and "They should all be fitted, sexy,
attractive, and/or revealing, similar to the original clothes". She chose the starter batch: tank top,
crop top, jeans, leggings, leather jacket, sneakers, ankle boots.

## What changes

- Seven new garments for the MetaHuman heroine, fitted as closely as the starter tank top and shorts:
  black rib scoop tank, crimson cropped top, indigo low-rise skinny jeans, charcoal leggings, black
  cropped leather jacket (Blender recipe `modern_wardrobe.py`), white canvas sneakers and cognac flat
  ankle boots (`footwear.py`).
- Appended `WearableDefinition`s (save-safe), new icon glyphs, and General Store Clothing rows after the
  period garments at Balance's prices (100–450 coins).
