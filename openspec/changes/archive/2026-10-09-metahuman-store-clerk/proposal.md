# MetaHuman Store Clerk

## Why
Jenny's backlog (jenny-muu7idr3-mphjdv, 2026-10-04): "Instead of the placeholder heroine asset, the
General Store clerk should use a MetaHuman model with period-appropriate clothing, and it should be
a man in his 50s or so."

## What
- The general-store shopkeeper, Mr. Josiah Trethewey, becomes a MetaHuman man of about 55, built in
  MetaHuman Creator from Epic's stock Walter preset (Unreal Engine EULA), with greying slicked hair
  and mutton-chop whiskers a shade greyer than the hair.
- He wears original 1850s shopkeeper clothes made for his body in Blender: linen shirt with the
  sleeves rolled, wool waistcoat, madder neckerchief (the only saturated colour), long work apron
  worn only at the hem and pocket, wool trousers and boots. One prop: a pencil behind his ear.
- He idles behind the counter with his palms on it (no clipping) and turns a little toward the
  heroine. The "(stand-in body)" label goes; the stand-in body stays as the fallback.
- Performance: one NPC, 2K textures, LODs synced like the heroine's, no groom simulation.
- Jenny approves a review pack before delivery; Balance OKs in-game screenshots.
