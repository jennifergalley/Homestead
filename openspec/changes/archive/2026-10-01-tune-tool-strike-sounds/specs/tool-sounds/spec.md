# Tool sounds

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The pickaxe rings on every strike
Every landed pickaxe strike SHALL play a stone ping, never the wood chop, on the strike clip's contact frame. The
strike that breaks the rock SHALL play the familiar ping a little louder.

#### Scenario: Three strikes on a rock
- **WHEN** she breaks a rock that takes three pickaxe strikes
- **THEN** each strike plays a stone ping as the pick lands
- **AND** the third, breaking strike is a touch louder

### Requirement: The billhook cuts canes audibly
Every landed billhook swing on brambles or a sapling SHALL play a cane cut (a slash, a woody snap and a leaf
rustle), varied between takes, on the hack clip's contact frame.

#### Scenario: Clearing a bramble thicket
- **WHEN** she clears a bramble thicket with the billhook
- **THEN** each swing plays a cane cut as the blade lands
