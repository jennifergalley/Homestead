## Why

Jenny's playtest feedback (2026-10-04): brambles, weeds and branches should come back around the manor
and farm so cleanup is an ongoing loop; foraging (berries, roots, branches, stones) should be its own
loop with distinct berry bushes and clear hints; and near-home felled stumps should be clearable.

## What changes

- Upkeep: each day a few cleared overgrowth nodes within 90 m of the manor regrow (4% per node, 8% on
  plot edges and fence lines, at most 4 a day; never on paths, doorways or the forecourt). Windfall
  branches (8% a day, at most 2 a day and 10 lying) land under the woodland trees; picked by hand for
  0.8 energy, +3 Branch, +1 Kindling.
- Foraging: +40 berry bushes (thickets of 3-5), +30 root spots, +30 branch piles, +20 stone piles in the
  woods, generated by `Scripts/Terrain/woodland_forage.py`. Existing yields and timers are unchanged.
  Berry bushes are a new wild red currant look, and the hints read "[E] Pick Berries" and "[E] Dig Roots".
- Stumps: the axe clears a near-home (<60 m) permanent stump to bare ground for 3.0 energy (scaled by
  tool tier), yielding 2-3 Firewood and 1 Kindling.

## Impact

Placement ids 583000-583199 (forage) and 584000-584199 (windfalls). `Serialize` output grows only when a
stump is cleared (felled section value 2) or a windfall wakes; `SimulationSaveVersion` is unchanged.
