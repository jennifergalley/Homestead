## Why
Jenny's UI polish pass (cohesion.md section 5): selection looked blue and off-palette, craft rows used system glyphs, the hotbar selection had no frame, key badges were plain, and copy drifted (store name, hyphens for dashes, date format).

## What changes
- In-palette selection outline, ink ticks and crosses on craft rows, a lifted and framed hotbar selection, ink keycaps in the hint font.
- Copy pass: Trethewey's general store naming, en dashes for ranges and clauses, "Mon, Spring 1, 1851" dates, "Eat Cornish pasty".
