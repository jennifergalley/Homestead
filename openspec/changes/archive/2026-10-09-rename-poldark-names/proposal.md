## Why

People and place names must not reference Poldark (family, business or estate names).

## What Changes

Pascoe's General Store and Mrs. Martha Pascoe become Trethewey's General Store and Mr. Josiah Trethewey (Balance-approved); the greeting says village, not town. Audit found no other Poldark-derived names in source or data.

## Impact

Simulation rules, tests and player-facing copy only; no save-format change.
