# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The village street is smooth and well worn

The street from the main road to the village square SHALL be graded smooth with a gentle cross-fall, SHALL read as a worn earth track about 4 m wide with a worn centre, and SHALL NOT have choppy bumps. The grade SHOULD stay at or below 10 percent where the fixed layout allows.

#### Scenario: Walking down to the village
- **WHEN** Jenny walks the street from the main road to the square
- **THEN** the ground is smooth underfoot and the track looks trodden rather than lumpy

### Requirement: The village feels lived in

The village SHALL have a well, benches, a granite sett apron, cottage gardens and store clutter, placed from generated data relative to the village layout so a rebake carries them over. Its flower beds SHALL favour bloom-heavy flowers that read as colour from about 10 m. Player-facing text SHALL call the place the village.

#### Scenario: Arriving in the square
- **WHEN** Jenny enters the village square
- **THEN** she sees a well, benches, setts, tended cottage gardens and coloured flowers, not a bare clearing
