## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Terrain-following public road
The public road SHALL follow the natural ground. It SHALL be no steeper than 1 in 5 outside the bridge
keep-out, SHALL have no long straight-graded earthworks, and SHALL keep its centreline and chainage.

#### Scenario: Walking down into the river valley
- **WHEN** she walks the road from the manor down to the river
- **THEN** the road descends with the hillside, with no raised causeway or deep cutting beside the ground

### Requirement: River road bridge
Where the road crosses the river, it SHALL hold a level deck at least 0.8 m above the water. A walkable
period wooden bridge SHALL span the channel, with railings she cannot step through.

#### Scenario: Crossing the river
- **WHEN** she walks the road across the river
- **THEN** she crosses on the bridge deck without stepping into the water, and the railings stop her at the edges
