# Proposal: Night is steady moonlight, no moonrise

Jenny (2026-10-10): when the sun sets, a second sun rises in the east and looks yellow before it
pales. Cut the moonrise entirely and light the whole night with a low, persistent moonlight until
the sun rises, so nights feel realistic and encourage sleep.

The moon is now one fixed, high, level light. It fades in with dusk and out with dawn and never
travels, so it can no longer redden near the horizon. The moon disc is hidden.
Night brightness keeps the shipped "Set A" numbers (0.2 lux ground moonlight, sky 0.3, -1 EV floor).
