# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Menus are aligned, readable, and place items exactly where she drops them
Menu panels SHALL share aligned edges and one selection treatment; the crafting grid SHALL use the full page width; map labels SHALL render without text shadows; the wardrobe preview SHALL show the heroine standing with no camera controls; and accent states SHALL keep text at 4.5:1 contrast or better.

#### Scenario: Reading a selected row
- **WHEN** she selects a Wardrobe row
- **THEN** its text is easy to read against the accent
