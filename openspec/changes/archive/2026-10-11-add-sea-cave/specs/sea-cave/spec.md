## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A sea cave to explore
The estate SHALL have a walkable sea cave in the cove bay's west cliff, reached on foot along the bay's sand,
with a chamber of about 20 x 15 m holding a shallow freshwater pool. She SHALL be able to walk everywhere
inside it that she can walk into and back out again, and SHALL never be stuck or moved out of it by the
fall-through rescue.

#### Scenario: Exploring the cave
- **WHEN** she walks from the foot of the cove steps along the west sand and into the cave mouth
- **THEN** she follows the passage into the chamber, walks round the pool (wading only a little way in) and onto the ledge, and walks back out onto the beach

#### Scenario: Saving in the cave
- **WHEN** a game saved in the chamber loads
- **THEN** she stands on the cave floor where she saved, not on the cliff above it
