# Design

## Decisions
- Rules live in `Simulation/HomesteadBeach.{h,cpp}`; numbers are Balance's (2026-10-10, docs/design/balance.md). Five appended `ResourceKind`s gather by hand (0.5 energy) and are ready again at the next 06:00 rollover (`Beach::NextMorning`), not a fixed regrowth.
- The campfire is a sixth `ResourceKind` placement. Its `readyAtHour` is the hour it burns out, so the lit state rides on the existing resource-edit save rows: no save-format or `SimulationSaveVersion` change. A lit campfire counts in `IsNearFire`.
- Placements are generated by `Scripts/Terrain/beach_detritus.py` from the settled coast (after the Map Agent's reshaping), in a claimed id range, with frozen ids. Decorative scatter appends claimed `EstateSceneryKinds` to `EstateScenery.bin`, idempotently, like `lake_path_plants.py`.
- Meshes come from Blender recipes (reuse-first); the three meals get prepared-food art and icons like the crop meals.
