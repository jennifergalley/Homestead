# Add Beach Gathering

## Why
Jenny's card `jenny-mv36t3cy-p7dm5i` (Next build): "Please add seashells, rocks, driftwood, and other beach detritus, both for decoration and plenty for gathering that refresh daily, including edible gathers like mussels or something believable from the beach or shallows, and let me prepare them as dishes or sell them at the General Store". Added the same evening: a static beach campfire she lights with driftwood; it burns a few hours, lights the sand like the lamp but orange and flickering, then goes out.

## What
- Walk the beach: shells, driftwood, pebbles, mussel beds and laver lie along it among decorative wrack, shell and pebble drifts, bleached logs and sea-pink flowers, and every find is back the next morning.
- Sell shells, driftwood, mussels and laver at the General Store, and cook steamed mussels, mussel and potato broth and fried laver (Meals, Well fed).
- Light the beach campfire with 3 driftwood at night; it glows orange and flickers for 4 game hours, cooks like a cookfire, then goes out and can be lit again.
