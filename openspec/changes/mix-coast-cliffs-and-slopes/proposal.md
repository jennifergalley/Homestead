# Mix coast cliffs and slopes

## Why

Jenny's map playtest, 2026-10-10: cliffs everywhere, a cove path down a cliff face, paths sunk or raised, grass in
squares and lines, cliffs missing past the map edge, an off-limits pocket she could fall into, a hard river/sea line,
an angled beach corner and a blotchy boulder. Balance: rock face on the cove cliff, gentler village street.

## What

About half the coast becomes grassy slopes to the sand (the cove's head and east side, Gull Sands); the cove's west
wall stays cliff. The cove path is a 1 in 4 path with two short flights (Jenny's pick). Footpaths lie flush. The coast
past the map edge is cliff. She can't walk off a drop. River froth meets the surf. Village street leaves the road
90 m further on. Details: Scripts/Terrain/README.md "Coast mix".
