# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Confirmed outcome-level rules for this round. Expand them when the round is fully specified.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Processing raises value
Processing machines SHALL turn raw goods into artisan goods over calendar time, and each artisan good SHALL sell for more than its inputs.

#### Scenario: Mill flour
- **WHEN** she mills wheat at the restored water mill
- **THEN** flour is produced after the milling time and sells for more than the wheat used
