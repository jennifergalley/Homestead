# Proposal

Round 9: the water mill and artisan goods.

## Why

Raw goods sell cheaply. Processing them into artisan goods raises their value, and the estate
river powers a mill.

## What Changes

- **Water mill** restored at the estate river's mill site. Grain becomes flour, and the mill
  later drives other machinery.
- **Dairy:** a butter churn and a cheese press.
- **Kitchen preserves**, including blackberry jam.
- **Cider press**, with an orchard planted on the estate.
- **Spinning wheel** (wool to yarn) and **loom** (yarn to cloth).
- **Processing:** each machine takes time on the calendar, and its outputs carry the input's
  quality.

## Reuse starting points

- Round 1's mill-site landmark and river.
- The workbench and crafting.
- Stardew Valley artisan machines (conventions only).

## Smallest useful result and first playable demonstration

Mill wheat into flour at the water mill, churn butter, and sell both for more than the raw goods.
