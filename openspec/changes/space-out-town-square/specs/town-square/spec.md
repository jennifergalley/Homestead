## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Open town square
The town SHALL have an open square of about 60 × 45 m. Its buildings SHALL either share a party wall or
stand at least 3 m apart. A 5.5 m street SHALL run clear of every wall from the main road's end into the
square, and the general store's door SHALL face the square with a clear walk to it.

#### Scenario: Walking into town
- **WHEN** she walks from the end of the road to town into the square and on to the general store
- **THEN** she follows a curving street into an open square ringed by terraces and cottages with lanes between them, and reaches the store's counter without squeezing between buildings

#### Scenario: An old save
- **WHEN** a save made with the old town loads
- **THEN** the general store keeps its id and stock, and its counter stands at the new store
