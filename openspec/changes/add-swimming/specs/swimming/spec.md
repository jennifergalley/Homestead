# Swimming

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Swim in fresh water
The heroine SHALL wade into fresh water and, past about waist depth, swim with a swimming animation, so she can cross
the estate lake and come out on any shore.

#### Scenario: Swim across the lake
- **WHEN** she walks into the estate lake from the landing and keeps going
- **THEN** she wades, then swims across with a swimming animation
- **AND** she walks out on the far shore

### Requirement: No tool work while swimming
While swimming she SHALL NOT use tools, gather, or fill the pail.

#### Scenario: Pail while swimming
- **WHEN** she presses the fill or use button while swimming
- **THEN** nothing is filled or used
