# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Tools are held and swung without hands or arms clipping her body
Idle carries SHALL close her fingers around the tool haft; axe and pickaxe swings SHALL keep the forward wrist within a natural range; scythe and hoe swings SHALL keep the tool far enough in front that neither hand nor arm intersects her torso.

#### Scenario: Hoeing a tile
- **WHEN** she hoes a tile
- **THEN** both arms stay outside her torso throughout the swing
