# Estate river

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The river has a source and meets the sea

The estate river SHALL rise at a visible spring, a pool with stones and froth at the head of its
valley, and SHALL run into the sea's shore wash at the cove. Neither end SHALL be square.

#### Scenario: At the spring

- **WHEN** she stands at the head of the river
- **THEN** the water wells up in a small frothing pool set into the bank among granite stones, and
  narrows into a rill downstream

### Requirement: The river sits in its channel

Along its whole course the river's water SHALL lie between banks that rise above it on both sides.
It SHALL NOT float over or tilt across grass.

#### Scenario: A side-hill reach

- **WHEN** the valley side falls away beside the river
- **THEN** a bank still holds the water, and the waterline is where the water meets the ground

### Requirement: The river is always present

The river SHALL NOT be spatially loaded. It SHALL be visible from the manor and the drive, and the
pail probe SHALL find it from anywhere on the estate.

#### Scenario: From the manor

- **WHEN** she stands at the manor and looks down the valley
- **THEN** the river is drawn along its whole course, and a HomesteadWater search finds it

### Requirement: The pail reaches the water

When she fills the pail at the river, the pail SHALL dip into the water, not stop short of the edge.

#### Scenario: Filling from the bank

- **WHEN** she fills the pail about a metre back from the waterline
- **THEN** she steps down the bank, kneels, and the pail goes into the water
