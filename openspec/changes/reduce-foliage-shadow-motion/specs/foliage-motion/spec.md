## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Calm shrub shadows
The estate's swaying shrubs SHALL sway gently enough that their shadows don't visibly crawl at a
standing camera. Their sway, their shadows and the camera-safe dither SHALL each be switchable at
runtime, for comparison captures.

#### Scenario: Standing by a bramble at noon
- **WHEN** she stands still beside a bramble hedge in sun
- **THEN** the hedge's shadow edge only shifts slightly and slowly, while the bushes still sway
