# Estate lake

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A lake up-left of the farm on the map

The estate SHALL have a freshwater lake on owned land, north-west of the farm (up and to the left on the
north-up estate map). It SHALL flood none of the farm, the drive, the river, the spring, the MVP woodland
zone, or any interactable placement.

#### Scenario: On the map

- **WHEN** she opens the estate map
- **THEN** the lake is drawn up-left of the farm, with a dashed footpath from the farm to its landing

### Requirement: A believable pool

The lake SHALL have an irregular natural shore, a cut bank with granite on its uphill side, a low turfed
bank on its downhill side, and grassy margins. Its water SHALL be still, with the colour of a peaty upland
pool, and the ground SHALL draw its waterline with no visible slab edge.

#### Scenario: From the landing

- **WHEN** she stands on the landing and looks across the lake
- **THEN** she sees still, dark water meeting grassy and stony banks, with granite on the far bank, and no edge of a water slab

### Requirement: Water for the farm

She SHALL be able to walk the footpath from the farm to the lake's landing and fill the pail there at the
real waterline. She SHALL NOT walk under the water; a wading limit stops her about knee deep.

#### Scenario: Filling the pail

- **WHEN** she stands on the landing with the pail and fills it
- **THEN** she kneels at the water's edge and the pail is filled, as at the river
