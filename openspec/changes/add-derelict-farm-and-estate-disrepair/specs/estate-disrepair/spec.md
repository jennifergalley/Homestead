# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Start the estate visibly neglected, with an old overgrown farm field to find and clear.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A derelict farm field lies near the manor
The estate SHALL have an old field of roughly 50-80 m a side near the manor, bounded by a crumbling timber fence with leaning, fallen and missing sections and a broken gate, showing the remains of crop rows, and densely overgrown with clearable weeds, grass, brambles and saplings.

#### Scenario: She finds the farm early
- **WHEN** the heroine walks out of the ruin's rear-wall gap on a new game
- **THEN** the farm's broken gate and fence are in view within about 20 m

#### Scenario: The farm can be cleared
- **WHEN** she swings the scythe or billhook at the weeds, grass and thin bramble inside the field
- **THEN** each one clears like the rest of the estate's overgrowth, and the iron and steel kinds show as teases

### Requirement: The starting estate looks neglected
A new game SHALL show debris and overgrowth round the manor and along the drive, while the on-foot routes to the standing room's chest, the salvage piles, the ruin's gaps and the drive stay walkable.

#### Scenario: Obvious clearing work
- **WHEN** she first steps out of the ruin
- **THEN** she sees clearable overgrowth and debris all round the grounds

#### Scenario: Routes stay open
- **WHEN** she walks from the standing room to each salvage piles and out along the drive
- **THEN** no debris blocks the way
