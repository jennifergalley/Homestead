## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** the table above; continuous sand from the cove onto the long beach; a ~25 ha skinny estate; a cropped world with natural edges; reserved, named outlines for neighbours and communal land.

**Non-Goals:** moving the manor core, the village, its street or road (the gentler-street card stays unscheduled); neighbour or communal gameplay; land purchase; the mine interior; new speeds or fast travel; resampling the 4 km landscape.

## Decisions

### 1. Carve the cove closer; don't warp or resample the world

The manor plateau stands 86 m above the sea and the coast is ~330 m south. A coastal warp or half-scale resample would move water, beach, river mouth, cove kit and hundreds of placements and still leave the same 86 m to descend. Instead `cove_bay.py` (new, run-once like `beach_belt.py`) lowers the cove's valley north-west of today's inlet to seabed and a sand fringe 15-25 m wide, so the head sand sits near (-458, -612), about 200 m from the manor, between steep grassed cliff walls. The fringe runs round the bay head, joins the beach belt's east end (y about -615) and the existing CoveBeach sand by the river mouth, closing the gap Jenny found. The river channel, its mouth cut and the beach belt are left as graded; `river_channel.py` re-seats the channel if the bay touches it. `Tests/EstateBeachTests.py` gains a check that one walkable dry-sand strip links CoveBeach to the belt's west end.

### 2. New cove route: path, then steep cliff steps

The 86 m drop inside 288 m forces steps. Restore today's cove route corridor from the pre-route heightfield (`c1e426df2^`, only cells that route changed), clear `coveRoute`, then run `cove_route.py` with new `CONTROL`: from the front door down the opening valley's centre line (between its overgrowth, which stays) to the clifftop near (-372, -610), then switchback flights down the bay's east wall to the head sand. Flights steepen to about rise 0.19 / going 0.28 (34 deg) so about 300 steps fit about 110 m of XY; the upper path may grade at up to 1 in 4. A landing every 20-25 steps faces the sea, with kerbs and rails on the drop side (the kit's existing pieces), and wildflowers on the cliff face if an existing flower kind suits (Balance: sea pinks). If the route can't meet 60 s, stop and report rather than cut further. The kit (`HomesteadEstateCoveRoute.inc`) regenerates from it.

### 3. Mine ruin to the clifftop west of the cove

`MineEntrance` moves to about (-410, -722) on a graded pad (reshape's pad recipe), with a woodland footpath from the manor's west side (lake-path style: graded bed, cleared scatter, worn ground, dashed on the map) and a clifftop link to the top of the cove steps. Map glyph and travel destination follow the anchor; the travel arrival is re-seated on the pad.

### 4. Smaller estate, retired placements

New `EstateBoundary` (proposal image, west edge at Y -880 at Jenny's request): about 32 ha gross, ~25 ha of land; the stage-1 village slit and pocket go away because the village is outside. Every placement generator drops rows outside the boundary or in the new bay (ids stay as holes, never reused, per "Generated placement safety"); a regression test proves every surviving id keeps its kind and position. Retired interactive trees become non-interactive scenery records at the same spots so neighbour land stays wooded. Only 66 fellable trees stay on her land (435 today), so about 100 existing scenery trees in her woods become fellable placements with new appended ids (same spots and look; Jenny approved). Woodland forage keeps 40 of 120 spots and is topped back up to about 65 inside her land with new appended ids (roots and berry bushes along her paths and woods, off the opening clearing; Balance). Every clearable node stays: all 6 salvage piles (tool heads), 32 rubble, 36 small and 14 medium stumps, 12 barrels, 11 crates, 4 rubbish heaps, 13 rotten planks, 6 slate heaps and 2 ruin timbers, about 4,900 coins at the balance.md section 6 values (day 1 needs 1,200-1,500). The 14 disrepair rows on the public road's verge past the new estate gate keep their ids under the public-roadside exception instead of retiring. The opening overgrowth's heading is frozen to today's value in `HomesteadEstate.cpp` so moving `CoveBeach` doesn't move it. `berries.py` gains a village-street clearance before any regeneration.

### 5. Reserved outlines on the map

Layout polygons with new prefixes: `Neighbour.Penhallow` / `Tregarthen` / `Polwhele` (Polwhele kept east of the river so the river and its banks stay common), `Common.VillageGreen` / `Allotments` / `ChapelSands` / `VillageGrowth`, and `ForSale.TopField` / `CarnWood` replacing the three old for-sale polygons. `ParcelsFromLayout` ignores `Neighbour.` and `Common.` polygons (not parcels). The map view draws them as faint dashed outlines with their period names ("Penhallow", "the village green", "allotments", "Chapel sands"); no "reserved" or "future" copy. In the world they are already finished land (woods, fields, hedges); the edge band gets denser wood where it's open.

### 6. Village additions (Jenny, 2026-10-10)

A footpath of about 150 m from the village square's south-west corner past the allotments to the river bank (graded bed, cleared scatter, worn ground, dashed on the map). Six small labourers' cottages strung along the road out of the village (road chainage about 470-700 m, about 11 m off its centreline, alternating sides), using the village's existing cottage kit and placement path, each on a small graded pad clear of the road and its verge forage.

### 7. Cropped world

`PlayableBounds` (layout rectangle, about X -900..560, Y -1360..160) drives: `bake_estate_map.py` (sheet rectangle in `T_EstateMap.json`; same 4096 px, so about 0.37 m per pixel), the map view's pan limits, and an invisible pawn-blocking edge spawned at runtime along its land sides, always behind dense wood, hedge or sea. No path she follows ends at it: the public road ends inside the sheet at a field gate with a milestone ("Penvose 9 miles", a made-up market town; Poldark's Truro is out), and walking into the gate shows "The road to Penvose – another day."; the river path ends at Chapel sands. The landscape, ocean and outer land stay as horizon.

### 8. Rebake order (one writer)

`cove_bay.py`, route corridor restore, `cove_route.py`, mine pad and paths, river path and cottage pads, then `river_channel.py` (no-op check), `beach_belt` test, `weightmaps.py`, `public_road.py`, `bake_ocean.py`, placement generators, `bake_ground.py`, `bake_estate_map.py`. The village carry-over scripts (`town_pad.py`, `town_path.py`, `town_layout.py`, `route_sights.py --bake`, `village_dress.py`) are re-run only to confirm no change, since the village and its road don't move. Editor: `apply_estate_heightfield` and `ApplyEstateWeightmaps` over the printed rectangles, `build_ocean.py`, `place_water.py`, `build_ground.py`, `ImportEstateMap`.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- About 300 cliff steps is a long climb; Jenny judges it in the review walk. Fallback: fewer, longer path legs at about 60-70 s.
- Carved cliffs can read artificial: noise the wall profile, paint cliff/rock by slope, inspect in PIE.
- A third of the placements retire: forage is thinner; Balance owns the top-up.
- Chapel sands is ~600 m from the village square; no coast lies nearer and the village is fixed. Only an outline now.

## Migration Plan

No `SimulationSaveVersion` or `bakeVersion` change in this lane. Old saves keep surviving ids; retired ids, old for-sale parcels and positions in the new bay or beyond the crop are stale. Recommend Jenny starts a new game on this build (test saves are disposable); Integration chooses any version handling. Rollback: revert source and assets together and keep the last good build on the shortcut.
