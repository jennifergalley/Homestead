# Apply Dark Theme Everywhere

## Why
Jenny, 2026-10-01 (dark-palette playtest): she loves the dark book, but it wasn't applied everywhere. The clock read dark brown on the dark calendar, the meter and coins panels and the hint card stayed light parchment, and a short "Not ready yet" toast sat off-centre.

## What
- The whole HUD follows the chosen palette: clock, date, weather, meters, coins, hotbar, compass, hint card, toasts and pickups.
- Hint and notice cards get an umber version in Dark (cream ink, gilt frame, terracotta errors) and stay paper in Light.
- Every text and background pair reaches at least 4.5:1 in both palettes. The table is in `pitch-dark-parchment-theme/design.md`.
- Toast lines are centred on their card.
