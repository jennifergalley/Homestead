# Proposal

Round 12: family reputation, town events and land purchase.

## Why

The goal beyond wealth is restoring the family's name. Buying back neighbouring land is the
largest late-game sink.

## What Changes

- **Family standing level**, raised by:
  - visible restoration;
  - fair wages and jobs;
  - selling quality goods;
  - friendships (gifts and the greeting track from round 1);
  - town events and requests.
- **Standing unlocks** shop tiers, better hires, invitations and eventually courtship.
- **Seasonal town events** and a notice board of requests.
- **Friendship tracks** with shopkeepers and townsfolk, plus ambient townsfolk with daily
  schedules.
- **Buy the for-sale parcels** shown on the map (woodland, a moor field, a second cove) as a
  late-game money sink. Their boundary, ownership and building rights follow.

## Reuse starting points

- Round 1's parcels, map and greeting counter.
- Coral Island's town rank and festivals (conventions only).

## Smallest useful result and first playable demonstration

Complete two town requests, attend the spring fair, and buy the neighbouring woodland parcel.
