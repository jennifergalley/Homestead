# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Confirmed outcome-level rules for this round. Expand them when the round is fully specified.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Standing grows from visible contributions
Family standing SHALL rise from estate restoration, fair wages, quality sales, friendships and completed town requests, and SHALL unlock shop tiers, hires and invitations.

#### Scenario: Complete a request
- **WHEN** she completes a town request
- **THEN** her family standing increases and the change is shown

### Requirement: For-sale parcels can be bought
For-sale parcels SHALL be purchasable. A purchased parcel SHALL become owned, SHALL permit building, and SHALL appear inside the boundary on the maps.

#### Scenario: Buy the woodland parcel
- **WHEN** she buys the neighbouring woodland parcel
- **THEN** it becomes owned, building there is allowed, and the map boundary includes it
