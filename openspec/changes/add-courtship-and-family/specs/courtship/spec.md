# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Confirmed outcome-level rules for this round. Expand them when the round is fully specified.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Courtship is optional and standing-gated
Courtship SHALL be optional, SHALL progress through friendship and events with a chosen suitor, and SHALL require a minimum family standing before a proposal.

#### Scenario: Too early to propose
- **WHEN** she tries to propose before reaching the required standing
- **THEN** the proposal is declined gently, with a reason that points toward standing
