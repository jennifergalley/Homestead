# Proposal

Round 11: mine pumping, the engine house and deep levels.

## Why

The mine's deeper levels are flooded. Historically, Cornish mines lived or died by pumping, and
the steam engine house is the big goal.

## What Changes

- **Water-wheel pump** drains the first flooded level.
- **Steam pumping engine house** restored on the clifftop, the big investment. It drains the
  deep levels.
- **Winding gear** and a **dressing floor**, for faster ore handling.
- **Deep levels** hold iron, silver and gold, and topaz, emerald, ruby and diamond pockets.
  Deeper means harder rock, which needs higher pickaxe tiers.
- **Floods and rockfalls** block tunnels until they're shored.
- **Hired miners** from round 10 work the veins she assigns.

## Reuse starting points

- Round 7's mine systems.
- Round 1's mine-site landmark and engine-house ruin.
- Period Cornish engine-house references.

## Smallest useful result and first playable demonstration

Restore the engine house, pump out level three, and bring up silver ore and a ruby.
