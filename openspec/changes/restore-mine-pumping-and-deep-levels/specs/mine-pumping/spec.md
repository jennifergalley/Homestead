## Purpose

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Deep levels need pumping
Levels below the shallow level SHALL remain flooded until the pump that serves them is restored.

#### Scenario: Before the pump
- **WHEN** she reaches the shaft to level two before restoring the water-wheel pump
- **THEN** the level is flooded and inaccessible, with a clear reason
