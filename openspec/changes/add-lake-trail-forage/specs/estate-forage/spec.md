# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Forage along the lake trail
The woods either side of the lake trail SHALL hold live brambles and wild roots in natural clusters, some within a
few metres of the path and some deeper in, never on the trail's walk width or in the lake.

#### Scenario: Walking to the lake
- **WHEN** she walks the trail from the farm to the lake
- **THEN** she passes brambles and root patches she can gather from the path on both sides

### Requirement: Wildflowers line the lake trail
Decorative spring wildflowers SHALL grow in drifts along the trail's verges and in patches on the woodland floor,
without collision, and SHALL NOT stand on the walk width.

#### Scenario: Spring walk
- **WHEN** she walks the trail
- **THEN** bluebells, primroses, red campion and cow parsley line it in drifts, with bluebells, anemones and wild
  garlic under the trees
