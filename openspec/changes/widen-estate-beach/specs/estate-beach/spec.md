# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A dry sand beach below the estate's cliffs
The estate's open south coast SHALL have a belt of dry, walkable sand along the foot of its cliffs, from the
west boundary to the cove, 17-38 m wide from the cliff foot to the swash line, shelving into the sea. The
cliff faces SHALL be left as they are and the river mouth SHALL stay open to the sea.

#### Scenario: Walking the shore below the cliffs
- **WHEN** she reaches the sand below the south cliffs
- **THEN** she can walk dry along the beach from the west boundary to the cove's headland, with the cliffs
  rising behind her and the sea running up the sand in front

#### Scenario: The map shows the beach
- **WHEN** she opens the estate map
- **THEN** the south coast shows a sand beach along the foot of the cliffs
