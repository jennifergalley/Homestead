# Proposal

Round 8: horse and wagon.

## Why

The handcart limits hauls on a 4 km map. A horse and wagon she drives herself makes bigger,
faster trips to town, with the horse's upkeep as a money sink.

## What Changes

- **Buy a horse** from the livestock dealer, and a **wagon** from the blacksmith or builders'
  merchant.
- **Stable** built on the estate. The horse needs feed and stabling, a daily sink, plus gentle
  care and bonding.
- **Hitch and drive** the wagon on the road with simple, comfortable controls on the
  controller and on the keyboard.
- **Load crates** visibly into the wagon bed, and unload them at each shop door.

## Reuse starting points

- The round-3 cart loading.
- Unreal Chaos Vehicles or a kinematic wagon.
- Licensed free horse animations where the terms verify.
- Round 1's road width is sized for a wagon.

## Smallest useful result and first playable demonstration

Hitch the horse, load twenty crates of produce, drive to town, and unload at three shops in one trip.
