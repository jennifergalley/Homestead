# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Confirmed outcome-level rules for this round. Expand them when the round is fully specified.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: She drives her own wagon to town
A purchased horse and wagon SHALL be drivable by the heroine on the road. Loaded crates SHALL be visible and SHALL unload at shop doors. The horse SHALL require stabling and feed.

#### Scenario: Drive to town
- **WHEN** she hitches the horse and drives a loaded wagon to town
- **THEN** she arrives at the town square and can unload at shop doors
