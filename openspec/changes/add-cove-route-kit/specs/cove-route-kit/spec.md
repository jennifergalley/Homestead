# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Climbable granite steps
The kit SHALL provide granite treads and a landing whose rise, going and width follow the route's
constraints, pivoted at the tread nosing, with a skirt that hides the cut ground beneath them.

#### Scenario: A flight on the cliff band
- **WHEN** Water's builder places a 10-riser flight at rise 16 cm and going 32 cm on the cut ground
- **THEN** she walks up and down it without stepping into gaps, and no gap shows under any tread

### Requirement: The drop edge stops her
Kerbs and rails on the seaward side SHALL block her. She SHALL NOT be able to step up onto a kerb, and the
camera SHALL pass through the rails.

#### Scenario: Walking into the rail
- **WHEN** she walks toward the drop along a railed stretch
- **THEN** she stops at the rail line, and the camera still orbits freely past it

### Requirement: Rails on either side
The rail pieces SHALL serve either side of the path, or both.

#### Scenario: A flight cut into the rock
- **WHEN** a flight has rock on the uphill side
- **THEN** it takes a rail only on the seaward side, from the same pieces

### Requirement: Fingerposts name the route
A fingerpost reading "To the Cove" SHALL stand at the manor-south gate and at the top of the cliff stairs,
its arm pointing along the path.

#### Scenario: Leaving the manor
- **WHEN** she walks out through the manor-south gate
- **THEN** the fingerpost there points her down the cove path
