## Why
Jenny (backlog jenny-mutdt417-zj976u): she lost the ability to chop down trees moving from the survival MVP to the estate. Only the ~435 simulation ForestTree placements can be felled; the 43,000+ woodland, hedge and avenue trees are scenery. She wants to chop any tree on the map, with the chop animation and sounds, and have trees in forests or far from the manor and farm grow back.

## What changes
- With the axe out, any standing scenery tree within reach can be felled: same three-stroke clip, chop sounds, topple and thud as today, 6 Timber and 4 Branch, 4 Energy (Balance-approved).
- Trees on the public road verges and in the town square can't be felled ("This tree is part of the village.").
- A felled tree leaves a stump. 60 m or more from the manor and the derelict farm it regrows: sapling after 1 day, a full tree after 3 days. Nearer the manor or farm the stump stays.
- Saved in a new optional trailing "felled" section (no save version change).
- Growth rates are deferred; regrowth is a fixed 3 days.
