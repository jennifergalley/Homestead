# Wardrobe: swap-equip, store clothing, refit

## Why

Jenny, planner card 2026-10-04: "Alternate clothes should be equippable without "unequipping" worn
items first. They also shouldn't be available in the manor chest when we start, but available for
purchase at the General Store when the game begins. I'd also enjoy it if all the clothing items fit
her frame about as well as the starter clothes, so everything is tight, fitted, and relatively sexy."

## What changes

- **Swap-equip.** The field book's equipment slots offer every carried garment that fits, even when
  the slot is filled; wearing one puts the displaced garment in her pack. With a chest open, a
  garment in it can be worn directly; the displaced garment goes into that chest.
- **Store clothing.** New games no longer stock the alternate outfit in the manor chest. The General
  Store sells the seven alternate garments (shirt, long shirt, trousers, fur coat, fur boots, woven
  sandals, turnshoes) from day one, under a "Clothing" section. Prices from Balance. Existing saves
  keep whatever they own; the save format is unchanged.
- **Refit.** Every alternate MetaHuman garment is refitted in Blender to sit as close to her body as
  the starter tank top and shorts, then re-imported.
