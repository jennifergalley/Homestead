# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Rucksack straps sit on her body
The rucksack's straps SHALL rest on the surface of her shoulders and torso and SHALL not pass inside her body while idle, moving or working.

#### Scenario: Rucksack straps sit on her body
- **WHEN** she walks with the rucksack on
- **THEN** the straps stay visible on her shoulders and chest
