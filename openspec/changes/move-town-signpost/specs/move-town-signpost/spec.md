# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The town signpost stands on the road past the derelict farm
The road sign that offers travel to town SHALL stand beside the estate road beyond the derelict farm, with its arm pointing toward town.

#### Scenario: The town signpost stands on the road past the derelict farm
- **WHEN** she follows the road past the derelict farm
- **THEN** she finds the town signpost beside the road, pointing toward town
