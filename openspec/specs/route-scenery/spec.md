# route-scenery Specification

## Purpose
Scenery points along the road between the manor and the village, so the walk has something to look at every so often.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Something to see along every route
Every walkable estate route SHALL have a scenery or forage point at most 50 m from the previous one.

#### Scenario: Sprinting a route
- **WHEN** the player sprints any estate route end to end
- **THEN** a flower patch, forageable or similar sight is visible at least every 50 m.
