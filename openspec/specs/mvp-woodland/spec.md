# mvp-woodland Specification

## Purpose
Part of the Estate keeps the look and feel of the MVP survival game's woodland.

## Requirements

### Requirement: The estate has an MVP woodland region
The Estate SHALL contain one bounded woodland region, reachable on foot from the manor in about a
minute, whose trees, understory, ground cover, granite and density follow the MVP survival game's
woodland, while the rest of the estate keeps its Cornish character.

#### Scenario: Walk into the MVP woodland
- **WHEN** she walks west from the standing room for about a minute
- **THEN** the Cornish scrub gives way over a short mixed fringe to small broadleaves and firs a few metres apart with the odd jacaranda, bramble thickets, toyon, deer brush, ferns, grass clumps and granite outcrops, as in the MVP

#### Scenario: The rest of the estate is unchanged
- **WHEN** she is outside the region
- **THEN** the woods, moor and pasture show the estate's own Cornish species

### Requirement: The MVP woodland can be gathered like the MVP's
The region SHALL hold interactable forest trees, branches, roots, berry bushes, stones and flowers,
visible and reachable, at a density that makes gathering there feel like the MVP.

#### Scenario: Gather in the MVP woodland
- **WHEN** she walks through the region with her tools
- **THEN** she finds fellable trees and branch, root and berry forage every few tens of metres, and no decorative scenery hides or blocks them
