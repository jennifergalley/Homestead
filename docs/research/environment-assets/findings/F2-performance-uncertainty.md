# F2 - Foliage and clouds need isolated measurement

Status: mechanisms supported; universal performance thesis insufficiently evidenced.

Epic documents WPO/masked foliage limitations, light/deformation shadow-cache
invalidation and cloud quality costs (S10, S14-S16). An independent 2023
practitioner reports a large WPO shadow-pass difference (S17), but it is old,
uncontrolled and not a whole-frame comparison. Multiple Epic pages remain one
institutional source; S23 is only a mirror of S17.

There are not three independent, differently typed sources establishing that
Nanite, conventional foliage or clouds will meet Homestead's 4K goal. No such
claim is made. Asset admission, static material correctness, density/LOD,
wind/shadows and optional clouds are separate gates. Published polygon counts
and 32 GB system RAM cannot substitute for imported-mesh and GPU measurements.
