## Sources and method

Use public publisher asset pages and license documents for exact asset claims;
official Unreal documentation for integration constraints; independent technical
or practitioner evidence for counterarguments where available. Parallel bounded
research covers trees/foliage, ground/dynamic sky, and license/performance risk.
No API keys, credentials, private uploads, downloads, claims or account changes.
Each usable source receives its own file with URL, access date, short exact
quotes and credibility/recency/bias notes. `sources.csv` indexes the corpus.

Broad conclusions require three independent, differently typed sources or an
explicit insufficient-evidence label. Multiple pages from one publisher do not
constitute independent corroboration. Publisher statements establish their own
license/offering, not measured game quality or compatibility.

## Risks and opposition queries

- "Free" may be expired, engine-restricted, account-gated or tied to entitlement.
- Scan assets may carry excessive geometry, poor leaf masks, no wind/LODs or
  incompatible material graphs. Search Nanite masked foliage/WPO/overdraw costs.
- A static HDRI can flatten or contradict the current day/night/weather system.
- Prototype tint helpers may erase real bark/leaf material separation.
- New blocking trees can invalidate clearing/building; pretty grass can hide
  pickable plants. Preserve gameplay authority over decoration.
- Importing new assets requires an approved offline-safe editor workflow; a
  tested Shipping executable alone does not authorize editor/commandlet launches.
