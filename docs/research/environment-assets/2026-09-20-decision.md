Later source-only refresh/acquisition evidence is in
`diffs\2026-09-20-delta.md`. The original planning observations below remain

## Distinct no-account fallback bundle

**Fir Sapling + Fern 02 + the existing Brown Mud Leaves 01/Rock Moss Set 02 +
existing dynamic sky.** Start without Grass Medium 01 or new clouds. Use sparse
fern pockets and open ground rather than retaining a carpet of pointed grass
primitives. Grass Path 3 remains the optional ground alternate below, not a
mandatory fallback download.

Exact alternate tree: **Fir Sapling**, not Fir Sapling Medium, by **Rob Tuytel
(photography) and Rico Cilliers (modeling)**:
https://polyhaven.com/a/fir_sapling (S28). Three small fir variations; current
free/CC0 declaration and the same no-account access policy. Blend, FBX, glTF and
USD with 1K/2K/4K/8K choices; branch and twig PBR, twig alpha/mask, DX/GL normals.
Published source polygons: **433,021**; declared 1K FBX: **19,037,468 bytes**
plus texture dependencies. Both source figures are lower than Tree Small 02,
but runtime triangles, LOD provision, wind, Nanite, UE materials and performance
remain unverified. Source S28 contains the exact publisher preview link.

**Tradeoff:** this is realistic **open young conifer woodland**, not equivalent
mature broadleaf enclosure. Do not enlarge young firs arbitrarily to fake mature
trees, or turn decorative saplings into gameplay resources. If the altered
silhouette no longer preserves the liked enclosing woodland, stop with an
explicit partial result rather than declaring the fallback a full visual pass.
The untouched accepted environment remains the rollback.

## Alternate ground and conditional marketplace option

**Grass Path 3** by Rob Tuytel
(https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_path_3, S08, S26-S27) is a CC0/free alternate worn-route
surface: compacted dirt/gravel with scattered grass, not the underwater bed.
It is not a mandatory third full-world layer. Its small repeat scale may become
obvious when tiled broadly (verified 1 m tile; 2K PNG diffuse/DX normal/roughness
available, up to 8K offered); use only if the grass/leaf blend needs a distinct
worn approach.

**European Black Alder** by Quixel
(https://www.fab.com/listings/9de7ce19-5813-42d2-a5f0-5e6447006f72, S06)
is **not currently admitted**. Its retrieved description offers 22 tree variants,
dedicated LODs and simple/pivot-painter wind, but price/license/claim fields could
not be verified; subsequent full-page retrievals returned 403. This is an evidence
gap, not a finding that it is paid. Preview terrain/extra scatter is excluded.
The 2025 announcement of five free tree packs does not verify this listing now.

## Exact integration consequences

The main fix is **material preservation**, not simply importing more geometry.
`AddDecoration` overwrites all source slots; `AddPart` overwrites slot zero;
the bootstrap currently disables imported materials/textures and combines FBX
meshes. Add a targeted authored-material/bounds/pivot route while keeping
prototype callers and existing rock behavior intact. Preserve bark/leaf slots,
DX normals, linear roughness/alpha, leaf two-sided response and mip correctness.
Keep root pivots on analytic ground and scale uniformly rather than stretching
tree bounds on three axes.

Keep seeded instancing, the protected home/structure/plot/resource areas and
separate resource base/produce lifecycles. New decoration must not refill a
cleared sapling site with an unremovable tree. Keep ripe berries/flowers and
weeds legible; resource rewards/cooldowns and terrain topology do not change.
Exact helper/line evidence is in `integration-evidence.md`.

Keep the current sun/moon/atmosphere/skylight/fog owner. No static daytime HDRI,
second lighting rig, imported showcase map or independent weather clock.
Native cloud material names/parameters are documented but not tested in this
installed patch. Clouds compete for headroom after foliage, not before it.

## Admission, provenance and redistribution

CC0 asset sources can be included with project work under the provider's stated
policy (S04), but the team still records creators, exact source/license URLs,
date, actual bytes and SHA-256, conversion steps and selected imported outputs.
Keep map/mesh licensing with the candidate and preserve known hash mismatch
failures. Do not generate fake receipts from page metadata.

Link publisher previews for review; do not ship their images under an assumed
CC0 license. The previews in individual source records were recovered from
metadata, not independently pixel-reviewed here.

Fab's official summary permits incorporated projects and collaborator sharing,
not standalone redistribution (S19-S20). The full binding agreement was not
recovered; no Fab asset is admitted on that summary alone, and raw public-repo
redistribution must never be assumed. Expired Megascans promotions and prior
unknown claims confer no verified entitlement (S21-S22).

The chosen Poly Haven route has no verified human account/checkout prerequisite.
The **human/coordinator blocker is engine authoring approval**: the parent-reported
Shipping listener check does not clear editor/commandlet imports. A future Fab
alternative would also require exact live license/price/source confirmation and
any human claim/EULA steps. This research performs none of those actions.

Homestead compatibility. The old independent WPO report supplies a concrete

## Next-run boundary

The design and 29 unchecked tasks sequence authorization/offline gate,
representative acquisition/import, Batch A, coherent integration, targeted
harvest/regrowth/clear/build/save/collision checks, screenshot-free timing,
Batch B and isolated parent-reviewed candidate handoff. Two bounded visual
reviews are the limit, not the start of an endless cosmetic loop.

Normal-Lit evidence must record actual Lighting on, Shader Complexity off,
requested 100% and runtime primary render fraction, actual 4K viewport, time/
weather and candidate hashes. Sampled screenshots are not a timing benchmark;
tick/framebuffer results do not establish physical scanout/VRR/60 Hz or resolve
the observed desktop 30 Hz/tearing issue. Technical passes are not Jenny's
aesthetic approval. Native clouds and Nanite are conditional measured options,
not mandatory feature checkboxes.
