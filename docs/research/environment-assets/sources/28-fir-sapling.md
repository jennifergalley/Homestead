## Retrieved facts and limits

Rob Tuytel: photography; Rico Cilliers: modeling. Branch maps include diffuse,
AO, roughness, displacement, DX/GL normal and ARM. Twig maps include diffuse,
AO, roughness, DX/GL normal, ARM, alpha and mask. Shared S04-S05 policies establish
commercial CC0 use and no-account ordinary download access.

This is the small Fir Sapling, NOT Fir Sapling Medium. Published source complexity
and FBX bytes are lower than Tree Small 02, but neither establishes runtime mesh
triangles or a performance gain. LOD provision is not advertised in retrieved
metadata; no statement that LODs definitely do not exist is made. Wind, Nanite,
UE materials, units/pivots and 5.8.2 compatibility remain unknown.

Young conifers cannot be arbitrarily enlarged and described as mature broadleaf
canopy. Keep decorative firs separate from keyed interactive sapling resources.
