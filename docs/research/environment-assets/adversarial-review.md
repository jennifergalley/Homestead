# Adversarial review

Date: 2026-09-20. Performed after independent bounded foliage, ground/sky and
license/performance investigations. This is a decision review, not an engine test.

## 1. What would make the preferred recommendation wrong?

The selected tree's source complexity could make conversion/LODs disproportionate
for an 80 m map, and published scatter polygon counts are not per-mesh budgets.
General-purpose CC0 sources can require more material work than an Unreal-ready
pack. A sparse scanned tree and worn grass may look less lush than the requested
cozy countryside. None of these possibilities is disproved by a publisher preview.
Therefore the primary recommendation is a representative-spike candidate, not
permission to acquire everything and fill the world.

## 2. What is the strongest alternative, and why not select it now?

European Black Alder describes dedicated LODs, sapling/seedling variants and
simple/pivot-painter wind (S06), which could reduce engine-integration effort.
However, accessible publisher content did not expose current price/license/
claim fields; a 403 is an evidence gap, not evidence that the pack is paid or
that it is free. The historical 2025 free-tree announcement (S22) does not cure
that gap. A verified no-account CC0 fallback is safer to schedule autonomously.

The existing world itself is the rollback, not the promised visual upgrade.
Keeping it temporarily is better than shipping bad realistic assets as a win.

## 3. Are costs or benefits being inferred from the wrong proxy?

Yes, unless controlled: aggregate polygon counts, file sizes, HISM component
counts and 4K PNG dimensions are tempting but inadequate proxies. Wind and a
moving sun can invalidate VSM caches (S16); the independent WPO report (S17)
illustrates the mechanism but cannot set this machine's millisecond budget.
Nanite can improve shadow work while masked leaf cards remain problematic
(S14-S16). There is no justified blanket winner.

Cloud screenshots can look attractive while their ordinary ground-level benefit
is small. Native atmosphere already exists. Clouds must earn a separate measured
place, and unchanged sky is a valid fallback (S10-S13). Exposure must not become
a workaround for wrong normals, overly bright albedo or missing material slots.

## 4. What could silently break despite attractive pictures?

Permanent tree colliders can occupy cleared/buildable space; dense decoration
can camouflage ripe plants; baked-in fruit cannot respect harvest/renewal;
anisotropic bounds normalization can distort trees; alpha mipmaps and WPO bounds
can pop during movement; grass can conceal terrain/root intersection errors.
The live map also changes when structures/plots are built. These require ordinary
movement, construction, save/reload and lifecycle checks, not just matched stills.

The most important operational trap is new asset import: a listener-free Shipping
game does not prove an editor or commandlet will avoid the reported TCP/firewall
issue. No render benefit authorizes unsafe tooling or firewall changes.
