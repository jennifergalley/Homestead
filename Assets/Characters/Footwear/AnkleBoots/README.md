# SKM_AnkleBoots

Flat leather ankle boots, sleek and laced to the bone: a close-fitting cognac derby boot built
like a Goodyear-welted dress boot, cut to hug her ankle rather than stand off it.

- **Upper.** Cognac aniline calf, polished, leather lined. Quarters lapped over the vamp, a
  backstay and a curved heel counter, all double-stitched in a tan thread a shade lighter than the
  leather; the eyestays are double-stitched round the lacing slit.
- **Cut.** The shaft ends clearly above the ankle bones: 131 mm lateral, 134 mm medial, 139 mm at
  the heel, 129 mm at the throat. The wall stands 1.7-2.2 mm off the skin up the shaft, so it hugs
  the ankle.
- **Last.** A designed round almond toe ending about 1.7 cm past her toes, about 3 cm tall at the
  toe and rising smoothly into the vamp (`modern.toe_box`). The bottom is lasted square all round
  below 12 mm (`modern.square_bottom`), so the upper drops straight onto the welt.
- **Sole.** A slim welt and sole that follow the upper closely (1.8 mm overhang median, at most
  3.7 mm), a dark welt stitch, a dressed and burnished dark-brown edge, a natural leather sole with a
  1.8 mm raised waist and 7.8 mm of toe spring (rocker from the ball), and a flat stacked leather heel
  with a black rubber top-piece. 9.5 mm under the foot.
- **Lacing.** Nine pairs of small antique-brass eyelets and a 2.6 mm waxed round lace in dark
  brown, criss-crossed and tied in a bow at the throat, over a gusseted tongue.
- **Wear.** Antique darkening at the toe and heel, soft flex lines across the vamp and the front
  of the ankle, a little dust where the upper meets the welt.

See `..\README.md` for the rebuild, the Unreal import settings and the mask convention.

**Known weaknesses:**
- **Rigid toe box and sole.** The toe bones are folded into `ball_*`, so the forepart and the
  sole bend at the ball as one piece.
- **Facets.** The sole outline and the very tip of the toe show slight polygon facets in
  close-ups.
- **Hand-placed lacing.** The lace crossings ride on the flaps and never tighten or slide.
- **Eyelets** are a dark-gold dielectric; the material set has no metallic channel.

## Material notes

- **Calf.** Cognac at about (0.15-0.22, 0.06-0.09, 0.02-0.03) linear, roughness about 0.42 with
  broad sheen variation; very fine pores; toe and heel antiqued darker.
- **Sole and heel.** Dark dressed edges, stacked lifts with alternating tones, a black rubber
  top-piece; a pale natural leather sole worn darker at the ball.
- **Lace.** Two-ply waxed cotton, roughness about 0.45.
- Generated by `shoemaking\leather.py` (`calf`, `calf_lining`, `calf_rim`, `welt_sole`,
  `round_lace`, `brass`).
