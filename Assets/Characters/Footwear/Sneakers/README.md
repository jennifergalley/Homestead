# SKM_Sneakers

Low canvas sneakers, light on the lanes: a slim women's vulcanised low-top in the Superga 2750 /
Vans Authentic family, cut close to her foot rather than built up like a trainer.

- **Upper.** Off-white 12 oz cotton duck (linear albedo about 0.60-0.66, never pure white) over a
  cotton drill lining. Lock-stitched collar, eyestays round the lacing, heel seam and a quarter
  lapped over the vamp at each side.
- **Cut.** Collar 40 mm at the lateral side, 46 mm medial, 58 mm at the heel and 75 mm at the
  throat, with a soft tongue standing about 2 cm above the throat.
- **Last.** A designed round toe ending about 1.4 cm past her toes, about 3 cm tall at the toe
  and rising into the vamp, with 7.8 mm of toe spring (the outsole rockers up from the ball).
- **Unit.** A vulcanised rubber unit swept round the upper: gum outsole with a diamond tread
  inside a smooth border, a white foxing tape that hugs the canvas up to 9.5 mm (11 mm at the
  heel), a navy pinstripe over a thin red line, and a white rubber toe cap over the front. A small
  navy rubber label sits on the heel.
- **Lacing.** Five pairs of nickel eyelets and a 7 mm flat cotton lace, barred across the lowest
  pair, criss-crossed up and tied in a bow with sealed aglets.
- **Wear.** Broken in: a washed-out tone drift in the duck, grime ground into the weave toward
  the toe, dust along the foxing line, a greyed and scuffed rubber toe, grime low on the foxing,
  soft flex wrinkles across the vamp.

See `..\README.md` for the rebuild, the Unreal import settings and the mask convention.

**Known weaknesses:**
- **Rigid toe box and unit.** The toe bones are folded into `ball_*`, so the toe box and the
  rubber unit bend at the ball as one piece and don't wrinkle.
- **Hand-placed lacing.** The lace crossings ride on the flaps and never tighten or slide.
- **Eyelets** are a bright grey dielectric; the material set has no metallic channel.
- **Facets.** The rubber unit's outline shows slight polygon facets round the toe in close-ups.

## Material notes

- **Canvas.** A tight plain weave evaluated in the flattened panel (13 × 11.5 threads/cm),
  roughness about 0.85, warm off-white stitching, punched eyelet holes.
- **Rubber.** White foxing at about 0.6 albedo; gum outsole (0.30, 0.17, 0.07) with dust packed in
  the tread grooves and the border worn smooth at the ball and heel.
- **Lace.** Braided flat cotton with fine ribs and clear-sealed aglets.
- Generated by `shoemaking\leather.py` (`canvas`, `canvas_lining`, `canvas_rim`, `vulc_rubber`,
  `flat_lace`, `nickel`).
